Cadence may use third-party processing services to deliver its functions. Such services receive only what is required to perform the requested operation.

Where Cadence accesses information through Google APIs, its use of that information adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Authorized users can revoke the application's access at any time from their Google account settings.

Retention

Information is retained for as long as the authorized user keeps the relevant connection active, and is deleted when the connection is removed.
