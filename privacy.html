Cadence privacy policy

Cadence is personal software run by its owner for the owner's own use. This policy describes what it does with data from Google accounts that the owner connects to it.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, Cadence reads and manages mail (Gmail), calendar events (Google Calendar), contacts (Google Contacts, read only) and files it creates itself (Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides) for the connected accounts.

Where the data goes

Data is stored on a server the owner controls, reachable only over the owner's private network. It is used only to provide the owner with their own assistant features: sorting mail, drafting replies for the owner to approve, extracting tasks, preparing meetings and writing a daily summary. Some text may be sent to an AI model provider under the owner's own account to produce those features.

What is never done

Data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models. Cadence never sends mail, creates invitations, shares files or deletes anything without the owner's explicit action.

Limited use

Cadence's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect an account inside Cadence, which revokes its Google access and deletes the stored sign-in, or remove Cadence at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

The application's support contact is the email address shown on its Google consent screen.
