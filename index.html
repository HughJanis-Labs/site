Cadence

Cadence is a personal assistant for one person. It runs on that person's own server, reads the mail, calendar, contacts and files of the accounts its owner connects to it, and helps the owner sort, draft, schedule and remember.

It is private software. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

Privacy policy

Google sign-in for this application is used only to connect the owner's own Google accounts to the owner's own server.
